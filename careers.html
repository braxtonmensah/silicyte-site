01 Why checkable

A model learns whatever its scorer rewards.

In Silicyte’s own published research, deliberately degrading a design loop’s scoring model made its reported gain 2.4 times larger (t = -14.60). That is why every task we ship comes with a key a program can score and a control that should fail, and why we test the grader as hard as the answer.

To see what the bench builds, read the free sample of four checkable biology tasks: each with its key, grader, controls and reward-hack log, graded in public on GitHub.

Biology work covers therapeutic and basic science only. We do not take projects that would make AI systems more capable of producing biological, chemical or other weapons.

02 Three bench roles

The work, as the bench was planned.

These roles are paused. None is open, and no terms or fees are on offer.

Science

Biology task author

Write graduate-level problems in protein and peptide science, microbiology, pharmacology and assay interpretation, each with an answer a program can check, and a control version that should fail.

For: a PhD or MS in a life science, or an equivalent research record.

Engineering

Verifier engineer

Turn expert judgment into code: graders, reference solutions and tests that score an answer the same way every time, plus the attempts that try to fool them.

For: strong Python and a testing mindset. A science background is a plus.

Data

Data integrity auditor

Find near-duplicates between training and test sets, label errors and silent preprocessing bugs: the class of problem behind our published leakage measurements.

For: data scientists and bioinformaticians who distrust a clean-looking table.
