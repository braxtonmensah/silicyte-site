01 Why this network

The grader can be gamed. So we check the grader too.

Most AI training work asks an expert for an answer and trusts it. We ask for the answer and the means to check it: a key a program can score, a control that should fail, and a record of who did what. A team training a model on your work can then trust the label, not only the person who wrote it.

This is not a slogan for us. In Silicyte’s own published research, deliberately degrading a design loop’s scoring model made its reported gain 2.4 times larger (t = -14.60). A model under pressure will find whatever its scorer rewards. So every rubric, reward signal and benchmark in this network gets attacked before anyone trains on it, and the people who attack it get paid like experts.

Biology work covers therapeutic and basic science only. We do not take projects that would make AI systems more capable of producing biological, chemical or other weapons.

03 How it works

One email to apply. Paid from the first hour.

  1. Apply. Email hello@silicyte.com with the track in the subject line, a CV or profile link, and one piece of work you are proud of.
  2. Screening. A short call and a look at work you have already done. No unpaid test tasks.
  3. Matching. When a funded project fits your field, you receive a written offer with its scope, hours and rate before any work begins.
  4. Paid work. Remote and flexible, paid for the hours you work at the rate in your offer.
