01 Why checkable

A model learns whatever its scorer rewards.

In Silicyte’s own published research, deliberately degrading a design loop’s scoring model made its reported gain 2.4 times larger (t = -14.60). That is why every task we ship comes with a key a program can score and a control that should fail, and why we test the grader as hard as the answer.

To see what the bench builds, read the free sample of four checkable biology tasks: each with its key, grader, controls and reward-hack log, graded in public on GitHub.

Biology work covers therapeutic and basic science only. We do not take projects that would make AI systems more capable of producing biological, chemical or other weapons.

03 How it works

Nothing to do until a project exists.

  1. Get notified. Email hello@silicyte.com with your field in the subject line.
  2. A project is signed. When a funded project needs your field, we contact the people who fit it.
  3. Written offer. Scope, deliverables and the fixed fee per accepted deliverable, in writing, before any work begins.
  4. Paid work. Remote. We set no hours; you choose how and when to do the work. Paid a fixed fee per accepted deliverable.
