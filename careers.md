# Silicyte Expert Network: expert judgment, made checkable

We are building a paid network of scientists, engineers and analysts who train and test AI systems. You write the hard
problems, grade the answers and catch the failures. Every piece of work you hand in ships with the evidence that lets
someone else check it.

Rates run **$100 to $150 an hour**, depending on the track and your expertise. Remote, flexible hours, independent
contractor. Apply with one email to hello@silicyte.com.

**How paid work starts.** Silicyte is early-stage and bootstrapped. This is an expert network, not a salaried opening:
apply once and we review every application; funded projects are offered at the stated rate, in writing, before work
begins. No unpaid test tasks.

## 01 Why this network

Most AI training work asks an expert for an answer and trusts it. We ask for the answer and the means to check it: a key
a program can score, a control that should fail, and a record of who did what.

In Silicyte's own published research, deliberately degrading a design loop's scoring model made its reported gain 2.4
times larger (t = -14.60; /papers/design-loops.html). A model under pressure will find whatever its scorer rewards, so
every rubric, reward signal and benchmark in this network gets attacked before anyone trains on it.

Biology work covers therapeutic and basic science only. We do not take projects that would make AI systems more capable
of producing biological, chemical or other weapons.

## 03 How it works

1. **Apply.** Email hello@silicyte.com with the track in the subject line, a CV or profile link, and one piece of work
   you are proud of.
2. **Screening.** A short call and a look at work you have already done. No unpaid test tasks.
3. **Matching.** When a funded project fits your field, you receive a written offer with its scope, hours and rate
   before any work begins.
4. **Paid work.** Remote and flexible, paid for the hours you work at the rate in your offer.
