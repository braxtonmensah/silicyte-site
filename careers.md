## 01 Why checkable

In Silicyte's own published research, deliberately degrading a design loop's scoring model made its reported gain 2.4
times larger (t = -14.60; /papers/design-loops). A model learns whatever its scorer rewards, so every task we ship comes
with a key a program can score and a control that should fail, and we test the grader as hard as the answer.

To see what the bench builds, read the free sample of four checkable biology tasks (/evals): each with its key,
grader, controls and reward-hack log, graded in public on GitHub.

Biology work covers therapeutic and basic science only. We do not take projects that would make AI systems more capable
of producing biological, chemical or other weapons.

## 02 Three bench roles, as planned

These roles are paused. None is open, and no terms or fees are on offer.

- **Biology task author.** Graduate-level problems in protein and peptide science, microbiology, pharmacology and assay
  interpretation, each with an answer a program can check and a control version that should fail. For: a PhD or MS in
  a life science, or an equivalent research record.
- **Verifier engineer.** Graders, reference solutions and tests that score an answer the same way every time, plus the
  attempts that try to fool them. For: strong Python and a testing mindset.
- **Data integrity auditor.** Train/test near-duplicates, label errors and silent preprocessing bugs (see
  /benchmark-leakage). For: data scientists and bioinformaticians.
