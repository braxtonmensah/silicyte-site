About Silicyte

Controls, measured
like results.

Silicyte is an independent research venture that measures whether the controls around automated work actually operated: the guardrails on AI agent fleets now, and the evaluations behind computational molecular design before that.

Founded by Braxton Mensah in October 2026. Both lines of work ask the same question: when a system reports that a check passed, did the check run, and does the number mean what it says?

01 Approach

Measure the control, not only the outcome.

A guardrail that never refuses may simply never have run. On one operator’s fleet of Claude Code agents, one guardrail was absent on 29.8% of the calls it existed to check, rising to 53.1% with 11 or more agents at once. The cause was process start-up; after the fix the guardrails were absent on 0 of 2,211 live calls. Read the measurement and its limits.

Candidate ranking helps determine which molecules receive experimental attention. We study how data separation, metric implementation and scoring choices affect that ranking.

In our own peptide-design experiment, withholding training families from a scoring model increased the reported computational gain. This is evidence of sensitivity to the scoring procedure. Independent experimental outcomes are needed to establish which candidates are biologically better.

The research record includes methods, controls, limitations and corrections so readers can inspect what each result supports.

03 Research practices

Clear scope. Traceable evidence.

Silicyte is at the research and prototype stage. We do not claim prospective laboratory validation of our designed candidates. The agent measurements come from one operator’s fleet on one laptop; they are not a survey of other fleets. Existing computational results and historical corrections are documented on the science page.

AI tools support software development and analysis. Their use does not establish scientific validity; reproducible methods, appropriate controls and experimental evidence determine what a result can support.

The browser checker processes selected files locally. It loads public website assets and examples from external servers. Any project work would require a separate agreement covering access, confidentiality and data handling.
