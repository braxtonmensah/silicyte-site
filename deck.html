The problem

Self-improvement claims are graded on the tests they were selected on.

  • AI systems now write the tools, prompts and code their next round runs on.
  • Published library-learning gains largely disappeared once compute was matched and reuse was measured (Berlot-Attwell et al., arXiv 2410.20274 and 2504.03048).

The study: Silicyte Ratchet (silicyte-0119, registered 4 Nov 2026)

400 sealed ARC-AGI-1 tasks (id list hashed before any run), a reuse gate, a knockout, a contamination gate, and a planted positive control that voids the run if the harness cannot see a real library. Pre-registration: github.com/braxtonmensah/ratchet. Result pending; our registered prediction is +2 of 400.

Why trust us

  • Our earlier headline, a guardrail hook absent on 29.8% of the calls it existed to check, came out 0.0000 on clean public machines. We said so on the homepage.
