New focus / Control integrity for AI agent fleets

Did your agents’ guardrails actually run?

Silicyte now measures control integrity for AI agent fleets: whether the guardrails on coding agents ran, covered every path, failed independently, and how often agents override them. On one operator’s fleet of Claude Code agents, one guardrail was absent on 29.8% of the calls it existed to check, and absence rose from 0.2% with one agent running to 53.1% with 11 or more. Our peptide and protein evaluation work stays on this site as earlier work.

Research note / 001

How sensitive is a design gain
to the model scoring it?

In our peptide-design experiment, withholding training families from the scoring model increased the reported computational gain. The result motivates closer evaluation of the scoring procedure; it does not establish better biological activity.
