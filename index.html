Silicyte

New medicines, designed from scratch by AI.

Silicyte designs de novo peptides and proteins that have never existed in nature, and predicts how human cells will respond to them before anyone runs the experiment. We are starting where the need is most urgent: antibiotics for drug-resistant bacteria.

The problem

Drug-resistant infections are one of the largest threats in modern medicine, and the antibiotic pipeline has nearly run dry. Classic discovery screens what already exists. Most of the useful chemical space has never been made, let alone tested.

Two engines

Design

Generative models that write new antimicrobial peptides and protein binders sequence by sequence, matched to the properties of molecules known to work.

Predict

Virtual cell models that forecast how human cells respond to a perturbation, so a target or candidate can be ranked before a lab cycle is spent on it.

Prove

Every model is scored on held-out data with pre-registered tests, and our designs go to independent wet labs for measurement.

Where we are

Our de novo antimicrobial peptide library is entered in the AMP Challenge 2027, where selected designs are synthesized and tested against drug-resistant bacteria by an independent lab in 2027. Our virtual cell models compete in the Arc Institute Virtual Cell Challenge. Silicyte was founded in 2026 in Bloomington, Indiana.
