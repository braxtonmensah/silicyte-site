Silicyte

Peptide medicines, designed from scratch by AI.

Silicyte is an AI platform for designing de novo peptides: molecules that have never existed in nature, built to bind a chosen target. Peptides sit between small molecules and antibodies, precise enough to hit hard targets and small enough to reach tissue that antibodies cannot.

Why peptides, why now

Peptides are now one of the most valuable drug classes in medicine. GLP-1 drugs are among the best-selling medicines in the world, and tumor-targeting peptides are the delivery vehicle behind radiopharmaceuticals, a field where large pharma companies have made multi-billion-dollar acquisitions. Yet most peptide discovery still starts from natural sequences and screens variants one at a time. AI can write candidates directly.

The platform

Design

Generative models that write new peptides and protein binders sequence by sequence, shaped to the properties of molecules known to work.

Predict

Virtual cell models that forecast how human cells respond to a perturbation, so targets and candidates are ranked before a lab cycle is spent on them.

Prove

Every model is scored on held-out data with pre-registered tests, and our designs go to independent wet labs for measurement.

Where we are

Our first de novo peptide library, designed against drug-resistant bacteria, is entered in the AMP Challenge 2027, where selected designs are synthesized and tested by an independent lab. We have also designed de novo protein binders against EGFR, a major cancer target, and our virtual cell models compete in the Arc Institute Virtual Cell Challenge. Next: tumor-targeting peptides. Silicyte was founded in October 2026 and is headquartered in South Jordan, Utah.
