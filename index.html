01 The finding

Three independent runs per arm, twelve arms in one allocation, job 12243941.

We starved our own scoring model. It reported six times the improvement.

A design loop is judged by how much it improves a held-out predictor. The theory is that a better scorer picks better molecules and so reports a bigger gain. We changed the one thing that should control that number, and it moved the wrong way.

02 The film

Above: a recorded run of our own membrane simulation.

Two membranes, the same peptides, and the peptides choose.

The bacterial surface is negatively charged and the human one is not, so the peptide's charged face is pulled harder to the first. By the end of the run they are buried in the bacterial sheet and still sitting on top of the human one.

It is a simulation we wrote, not a measurement, and it stops short of the step that actually kills the cell. Every lipid is tethered to its own lattice site, so the sheet springs back and no pore ever opens. Selectivity, the ratio between those two outcomes, is the number this company exists to predict, and it is measured in the science, not here.
