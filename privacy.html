Privacy

What we collect, and why.

Silicyte is a working brand of Braxton Mensah, an individual in South Jordan, Utah, United States. It is not incorporated. He is the person responsible for any personal information described here.

01 When you contact us

Only what you send, used only to reply.

If you email hello@silicyte.com, or fill in a Silicyte form (for example a LinkedIn lead form), we receive what you provide: typically your name, work email, company, job title and your answer to one question about what you need. We use it only to reply to you and to discuss the work you asked about.

We do not sell it, rent it, or share it with anyone for marketing. We do not add you to a mailing list unless you ask to be added. Forms are offered to people in the United States and Canada only.

02 When you visit the site

A cookieless page counter and web fonts.

The site counts page views with GoatCounter, which does not set cookies. Pages load fonts from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address when a page loads. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which keeps its own server logs. The browser checker on this site processes the files you select inside your browser; they are not uploaded to us.

03 Your choices

Ask, and it is deleted.

To see, correct or delete what we hold about you, email hello@silicyte.com with "Privacy request" in the subject. We act on it within 30 days. We keep correspondence only as long as it is useful for the conversation it belongs to.
