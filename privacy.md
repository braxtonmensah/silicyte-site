# Privacy

Silicyte is a working brand of Braxton Mensah, an individual in South Jordan, Utah, United States. It is not
incorporated. He is the person responsible for any personal information described here.

## 01 When you contact us

If you email hello@silicyte.com, or fill in a Silicyte form (for example a LinkedIn lead form), we receive what you
provide: typically your name, work email, company, job title and your answer to one question about what you need. We
use it only to reply to you and to discuss the work you asked about. We do not sell it, rent it, or share it with
anyone for marketing. We do not add you to a mailing list unless you ask. Forms are offered to people in the United
States and Canada only.

## 02 When you visit the site

The site counts page views with GoatCounter, which does not set cookies. Pages load fonts from Google Fonts, so Google
receives your IP address when a page loads. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which keeps its own server logs. The
browser checker processes the files you select inside your browser; they are not uploaded to us.

## 03 Your choices

To see, correct or delete what we hold about you, email hello@silicyte.com with "Privacy request" in the subject. We
act on it within 30 days. Last updated 26 October 2026.
